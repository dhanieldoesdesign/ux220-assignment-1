# Research / Reflection

In the article below, it's suggested that students who list their learning goals are able to more effectively regulate and achieve their goals. Therefore, by listing my own goals above, regardless of how related they may seem to one another, I'm more able to achieve them. Listing the goals out like this may also reveal connections between the goals. For example, learning a new programming language directly relates to getting hired at a game design firm or studio, and creating my own game could be the reason why I get hired. There's little to no correlation between my Professional, Financial, and Educational goals with my Personal goals. My personal goals seem to just be things that I eventually want to do, and don't directly affect the rest of my three categories. That being said, lived experience may potentially unlock a source of inspiration and creativity that I otherwise wouldn't have if it weren't for these bucket-list items.

Overall, the activities and goals listed above have some sort of relation with one another, and as the article suggests, listing them will help me self-regulate and achieve my goals more efficiently.

**Using e-portfolio for learning goal setting to facilitate self-regulated learning of high school students.** [(Link to article)](https://www-tandfonline-com.libproxy.wlu.ca/doi/full/10.1080/0144929X.2018.1496275#abstract)\
